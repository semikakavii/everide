Purpose

Built for the people who take the bus
every single day.

For millions of Sri Lankans, the bus is how the day begins. Yet the biggest part of the journey isn't the ride — it's the not knowing. Everide is being designed to change that, one stop at a time.

01

Know what's coming before you leave.

02

Spend less time standing at the roadside.

03

Make the bus feel like a real option.

How it works

Three steps to a calmer commute.

01

Check your stop

Open Everide and see the routes that pass the stop closest to you — where you actually are, not where an app thinks you should be.

02

Understand the next arrival

See an estimated arrival window for the next bus on a route, so you know whether to wait, walk, or come back in ten minutes.

03

Plan your time with confidence

Use those estimates to decide when to leave — no more sprinting for a bus, no more standing around for forty minutes.

Why it matters

Less guessing. More of your day.

Less uncertainty while waiting

The hardest part of taking the bus isn't the ride — it's the waiting. Everide is built to shrink that uncertainty down to something manageable.

Easier access to route information

Route numbers, stops and destinations, laid out clearly. Understand the route you already take, and discover ones you didn't know existed.

A future shaped by commuters

Beyond arrival estimates, we want to explore useful passenger-submitted updates and better information for both government and private buses.

The bigger vision

A country that moves with
a little more certainty.

Better public transport information isn't just convenient — it changes how people plan their lives. When the next bus is something you can reasonably predict, commuting stops being a daily gamble.

Everide begins with the simplest piece: knowing when a bus is likely to arrive at your stop. From there, the ambition is to help everyday mobility in Sri Lanka feel more dependable, more legible and more human.
